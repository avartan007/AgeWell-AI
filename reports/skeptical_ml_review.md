# Skeptical Machine-Learning Research Reviewer Audit (7-Point Protocol & Code Verification)

| # | Audit Dimension | Severity | Evidence in Repository & Protocol | Concrete Correction / Safeguard Implemented |
| :- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Data Leakage & Invalid Train/Test Splits** | **Critical** | Longitudinal cohorts have repeated observations per participant across waves. Row-level random splitting places Wave 1 of Participant $i$ in train and Wave 2 in test (`h3_split_leakage_audit.csv`). | Enforced `create_participant_grouped_temporal_split()` in `src/agewell/preprocessing.py` and verified via `tests/test_no_leakage.py` (asserting disjoint `participant_id` sets and strict wave chronology $t_{\text{test}} > t_{\text{train}}$). |
| **2** | **Unsupported Novelty Claims** | **Major** | Claiming "no one has used missing indicators or ML for ADL decline" would be false given Sperrin et al. (2020), Sisk et al. (2023), and Lu, Sato, Kondo et al. (2023) on JAGES. | Reframed contribution as an empirical investigation of **cross-wave transportability of informative missingness indicators coupled with post-hoc calibration and selective triage** across HRS and JAGES. |
| **3** | **Missing or Unfair Baselines** | **Moderate** | Comparing a calibrated missing-aware tree model only against an uncalibrated linear model conflates model family, indicators, and calibration. | Evaluated a full $3 \times 2 \times 3$ factorial grid (`Logistic Regression`, `Random Forest`, `HistGradientBoosting` $\times$ `Imputation-Only vs. +Indicators` $\times$ `Uncalibrated, Sigmoid, Isotonic`) in `h2_calibration_metrics.csv`. |
| **4** | **Incorrect Calibration or Abstention Methodology** | **Major** | Fitting Platt/Isotonic calibration on test data leaks prevalence; reporting abstention accuracy without coverage hides rejection burden. | `PostHocCalibrator` fits strictly on `val_df`. `evaluate_selective_abstention()` explicitly reports `actual_coverage`, `abstention_rate`, `retained_sensitivity`, and `retained_false_negative_rate`. |
| **5** | **Confounding, Missing-Data Assumptions & Subgroups** | **Major** | Missing indicators ($M_{ij}$) capture survey collection protocols (e.g., gait test skipped due to safety), NOT causal biological mechanisms, and can fail if survey skip rules change across waves. | Added MCAR vs. MAR vs. MNAR ablation (`h1_missingness_ablation.csv`) showing indicators slightly hurt RF under pure MCAR (`0.7255 → 0.7167`), and added disaggregated subgroup audits (`subgroup_fairness_audit.csv`). |
| **6** | **Code-to-Report Fidelity & Synthetic Disclaimer** | **Critical** | Presenting synthetic fixture metrics as real clinical discoveries is scientific misconduct. | Embedded `"synthetic_benchmark": true` and an explicit disclaimer in `experiment_results_summary.json`, `dashboard.py`, and all notebooks/reports. |
| **7** | **Experiments Needed for Master's Thesis Proposal** | **Moderate** | Before clinical claims in Japan, the pipeline requires external validation on real credentialed cohorts and competing-risks handling for mortality. | Defined a realistic 4-semester Master's plan: Semesters 1–2 on public RAND HRS (including mortality/attrition sensitivity), Semesters 3–4 on JAGES / municipal LTCI records under supervisor guidance. |
